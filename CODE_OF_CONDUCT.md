# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free place for students,
educators, maintainers, and contributors of every background and experience
level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still correcting inaccurate or unsafe information.
- Focus criticism on claims, evidence, and repository content—not people.
- Respect privacy. Never request or publish student IDs, enrollment documents,
  private email addresses, account credentials, or payment details.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, affiliate relationships, or employment by an
  offer provider when relevant.
- Accept moderation decisions and help de-escalate disagreements.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Spam, referral-code promotion, deceptive links, impersonation, or malware.
- Instructions for forged documents, fake education accounts, location
  bypasses, account resale, or other verification abuse.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer asks for a behavior to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report ordinary conduct concerns to the maintainers through the repository's
moderation or reporting controls. If a report contains sensitive information,
do not open a public issue; use a private repository security or abuse-reporting
channel when available.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. They will
consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.
