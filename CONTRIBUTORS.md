# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who researches, verifies, corrects, reviews, and
maintains the Student Offers Repository.

Git history is the authoritative contribution record. Contributors may also
add themselves here in the same pull request as a substantive catalog or
documentation improvement.

## Listing format

Add one alphabetical entry using a name or public handle you are comfortable
publishing:

```md
- [Public name or handle](https://github.com/profile) — research, verification,
  documentation, or maintenance
```

Do not add email addresses, school names, student identifiers, or another
person without their permission. A listing is optional and does not affect
credit under the repository license.
