# Governance

## Project model

The Student Offers Repository is maintained as an evidence-first community
catalog. Maintainers make final editorial and release decisions, while anyone
may propose additions and corrections.

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Protect students from deceptive links, surprise billing, and privacy risks.
- Require authoritative sources for active-offer claims.
- Review contributions consistently and disclose conflicts of interest.
- Keep ended offers clearly separated from active offers.
- Maintain repository automation, release notes, and community standards.
- Respond to conduct and security reports with appropriate confidentiality.

## Decision principles

When sources conflict, the order of preference is:

1. Current provider terms or the provider's official offer page.
2. Current provider help, pricing, or education documentation.
3. An authoritative program partner such as GitHub Education.
4. Reputable secondary reporting for context only.

Safety and clarity take priority over the number of entries. A maintainer may
remove or quarantine a listing until its terms can be verified.

## Changes and reviews

- Small corrections may be merged after one maintainer review.
- New offers and material eligibility or billing changes should include the
  completed evidence checklist in the pull request template.
- Policy, license, governance, or automation changes should receive additional
  review when more than one maintainer is active.
- A maintainer with a direct commercial interest should disclose it and, when
  practical, ask another maintainer to review.

## Becoming a maintainer

Consistent contributors may be invited after demonstrating accurate sourcing,
respectful reviews, and reliable maintenance. Access is granted gradually and
may be removed for inactivity, security needs, or violations of project policy.

## Appeals

Contributors may ask for a decision to be reconsidered by adding new evidence
or explaining which project rule was applied incorrectly. Repeating the same
argument without new evidence does not require another review.
