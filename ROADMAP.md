# Roadmap

This roadmap describes maintenance priorities, not guaranteed delivery dates.

## Now

- Keep high-impact AI, cloud, developer, design, and productivity offers
  current.
- Improve country and school-level eligibility notes.
- Move expired promotions to the ended-offers archive quickly.
- Enforce consistent billing and auto-renewal warnings.

## Next

- Add more regional guides with official local pricing and terms.
- Add provider-level last-checked metadata that can be reviewed in batches.
- Develop a lightweight duplicate and table-schema validator.
- Expand accessibility, education, and academic research software coverage.

## Later

- Publish versioned data exports derived from the Markdown catalog.
- Add optional machine-readable offer records without making Markdown harder
  to contribute to.
- Track scheduled offer deadlines and recurring verification windows.

Proposals are welcome through an issue. Each proposal should explain the
student benefit, maintenance cost, and reliable source strategy.
