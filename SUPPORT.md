# Support

## Questions this repository can help with

- Finding the official page for a listed offer.
- Understanding the catalog's status, eligibility, region, or billing labels.
- Correcting an inaccurate row or reporting a dead link.
- Proposing a new verified student offer.

For catalog questions, open a GitHub issue and include the provider, country,
school level, official source, and date checked. Do not attach private
verification documents.

## Questions only the provider can answer

Contact the provider or your institution directly for:

- Rejected student verification.
- Account access, subscriptions, refunds, or cancellation.
- Eligibility decisions for a particular school or country.
- Missing credits, licenses, or benefits after activation.
- Tax, payment, identity, or legal questions.

The maintainers cannot access provider accounts or override eligibility
decisions.

## Before opening an issue

1. Read the [claim guide](guides/CLAIM-GUIDE.md).
2. Check [billing safety](guides/BILLING-SAFETY.md).
3. Open the provider's official source linked in the catalog.
4. Remove personal information from screenshots and text.

Security-sensitive reports belong in the private process described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
