# Style Guide

## Voice

Write for a student who wants a quick, safe answer. Prefer short factual
sentences, plain language, and direct warnings over promotional wording.

## Markdown

- Use one level-one heading per file.
- Use sentence case for headings.
- Add blank lines around headings, lists, tables, and fenced blocks.
- Use relative links for repository files and absolute HTTPS links for external
  sources.
- Give every link meaningful text; avoid “click here.”
- Keep tables readable in source form and do not place multiple offers in one
  row.
- Use ISO dates (`YYYY-MM-DD`) and UTC when a time is needed.

## Offer language

- Prefer `free for 12 months` over `one year free!!!`.
- Prefer `payment method required; auto-renews unless cancelled` over
  `completely free` when billing follows.
- Say `available in listed countries` instead of `worldwide` unless the source
  explicitly supports worldwide availability.
- Separate `verified by official source` from `personally tested`.
- Name the audience: university, college, K–12, vocational, educator, or
  institution.

## Links and citations

- Put the official source in the offer row.
- Put research and supporting links in `SOURCES.md`.
- Remove marketing and analytics query parameters when the clean page works.
- Do not use affiliate, referral, shortened, or user-specific URLs.
- Archive an ended offer in `offers/ENDED-OFFERS.md`; do not silently delete
  useful historical context.

## Privacy and accessibility

- Never publish student documents, private school emails, account IDs, or
  payment details.
- Avoid instructions that require screenshots when text is sufficient.
- Expand unfamiliar acronyms on first use.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate status.
