# How to Claim Student Offers

## Prepare once

Keep these available before starting verification:

- Your official institution email address.
- A current student ID showing your name and institution.
- An enrollment letter, timetable, tuition receipt, or portal screenshot showing the current term.
- Your legal name written consistently across the provider account and document.
- A personal email for accounts that should survive graduation; use the school email only where required.

Do not upload more personal data than the verification flow requests. Redact unrelated financial identifiers when the provider permits it.

## Verification routes

| Route | Typical providers | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| Academic email link | Notion, iAsk, Beautiful.ai | A message sent to your school inbox; international domains may need manual review |
| SheerID | Google, Kiro, Spotify, some other plans | Institution search plus document upload if instant verification fails |
| GitHub Education | GitHub and GitHub Pack partners | GitHub account, school selection, academic email, and enrollment evidence |
| Application form | Miro, Framer, Granola | Manual review; answer intended-use questions accurately |
| Institution provisioning | Claude for Education, Microsoft 365 Education, some Canva plans | Your school administrator enables the account; students cannot self-approve |

## Recommended claim order

1. Claim the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack) first if you code. One verification unlocks many partner offers.
2. Claim no-card, while-enrolled plans such as JetBrains, Notion, and Figma.
3. Claim fixed-period tools only when you are ready to use them.
4. Delay cloud credits until a project needs deployment; the expiry clock usually starts at activation.
5. Claim card-required trials last, after adding calendar reminders.

## When verification fails

- Confirm that the document shows a current date or academic term.
- Check that account name and document name match.
- Try the provider's document route instead of repeatedly resubmitting an email.
- Ask the provider to add your institution or domain.
- Do not create multiple accounts to bypass a rejection.
- Do not buy “verified” student accounts; they can be revoked and expose your data.

## After approval

- Save the approval email and terms shown during activation.
- Note the exact expiry and renewal price.
- Export important work before graduation or plan expiry.
- Re-verify only through the provider's official account page.
