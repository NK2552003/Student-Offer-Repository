## Before trusting an old promotion

1. Ignore the date of a repost; find the original provider page.
2. Confirm that the claim or verification button still opens a live flow.
3. Read the current plan name. A provider may replace a benefit without removing every old article.
4. Prefer the newest provider announcement or terms page over screenshots and coupon sites.
