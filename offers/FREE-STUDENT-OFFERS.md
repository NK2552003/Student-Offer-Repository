## Important limitations

- **Education does not mean commercial rights.** Autodesk explicitly limits education licenses to educational purposes. Other providers may impose similar limits.
- **A credit is not an unlimited plan.** Monitor AI, API, and cloud usage. Stop or delete resources before credit expiry.
- **Institution-gated plans are not individually claimable.** Claude for Education and some Microsoft/Canva plans depend on your school.
- **School email rules differ.** Some services accept documents; some accept only `.edu`; others accept international academic domains.
- **Ordinary no-cost tools are labeled.** Visual Studio Community, Red Hat's individual subscription, and MATLAB Online Basic are useful to students but are not exclusive student promotions.
- **"(unverified 2026-09-11)" means the link is live but the terms could not be re-confirmed automatically** (bot-block, JS-only page, or SSO wall) during the latest review pass. Treat the listed terms as last-known-good and recheck in-browser before claiming.
- See the "Newly verified free plans" and "Needs manual verification" sections below for offers found after 2026-08-21 that are not yet folded into the tables above.
