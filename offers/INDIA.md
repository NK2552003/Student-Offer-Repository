## If your Indian college email is rejected

1. Check whether the provider accepts enrollment documents instead of email-only verification.
2. Use an official institutional email such as the one issued by your university; never fabricate a `.edu` address.
3. Ask support to recognize the institution. For Notion, verify that the institution appears in the World Higher Education Database.
4. For GitHub Education, submit a clear current ID, enrollment letter, or fee receipt showing institution and date when requested.
5. Avoid VPN-based claiming. Region mismatch can violate terms and can cause later billing or account review problems.
