## Before activating any trial

1. Screenshot the final checkout page, including the renewal price.
2. Record the activation date, expiry date, and cancellation route.
3. Set two reminders: seven days before expiry and one day before expiry.
4. For cloud services, delete paid resources and confirm billing is zero before the credit expires.
5. Never assume “student verification” prevents auto-renewal.
